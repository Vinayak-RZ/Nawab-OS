# B2B cold email

## Structure
1. **Subject** — specific, lowercase-friendly, no clickbait. Reference their world (sector, city, role).
2. **Opener** — one concrete observation (from research), not "Hope you're well."
3. **Bridge** — one sentence on why you might be relevant (no feature dump).
4. **Proof** — only real, verifiable proof from sender context. Never invent logos or metrics.
5. **CTA** — single low-friction ask (15-min call, reply with interest, forward to owner).

## Rules
- Max 5 sentences in body.
- One idea per email.
- India SMB tone: direct, respectful, no American hype.
- No attachments in first touch.
- Falsifiable claims only.

## Anti-patterns
- "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "synergy"
- Multiple CTAs
- Long paragraphs
- Fake personalization ("I loved your website")
