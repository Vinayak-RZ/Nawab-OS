# Sales discovery (warm follow-up)

## When to use
After a reply or meeting is booked — not in first cold touch.

## SPIN-lite questions
- **Situation** — "How are you handling [problem area] today?"
- **Problem** — "What's the friction when [specific workflow]?"
- **Implication** — "If that stays manual, what does it cost you per month?"
- **Need-payoff** — "If we could [concrete outcome], would that be worth a pilot?"

## Rules
- One question per message.
- Listen-first framing in email follow-ups.
- Tie back to research signals from vault/CRM notes.
- Never interrogate — conversational curiosity.
